    About us

    Your Trusted Partner for Earthmoving Solutions

    Perfect TeeraWorks is an earthmoving and equipment rental business based in Nanganur, near Nipani, Karnataka.

    We specialise in providing JCB 3DX Plus backhoe loader rental services for agricultural, residential, commercial, infrastructure and civil construction requirements. Our focus is on providing reliable equipment, professional machine operation and transparent business practices.
